Tree Removal in Googong

Tree removal should solve a specific property or safety problem, not simply be recommended because a tree exists.

Common reasons a customer may request an assessment include a dead or seriously damaged tree, unstable growth, a tree that has become unsuitable for its location, planned landscaping or construction work, storm damage or a tree occupying space that needs to be safely cleared.

Flora's broader tree-removal capability includes simple through complex removals, dangerous and storm-damaged trees and restricted-access work. Whether an unwanted tree is interfering with your yard, a damaged tree needs assessment or you need pruning rather than complete removal, our insured arborist team can inspect the site and recommend the appropriate work.

Before work begins, the site should be considered as a whole: the tree's condition and size, access for workers and equipment, neighboring property, fences, structures, landscaped areas and the space available for controlled removal.

Where there is limited room to bring a tree down in one piece, staged dismantling may be required. Where removal is not necessary, pruning may provide a better result.

NSW Tree Regulations

Googong Tree Rules: NSW and QPRC Requirements Apply

NSW rules apply here — not ACT

Googong is in New South Wales under Queanbeyan-Palerang Regional Council rather than ACT tree-protection rules. ACT size thresholds do not apply to Googong properties. QPRC's private-tree guidance specifically addresses Queanbeyan, Googong and Jerrabomberra and directs property owners to the relevant local planning controls.

Step 1

Check Your Property

Confirm the property is in the QPRC local government area and identify the planning controls applicable to the specific address and proposed work.

Step 2

Confirm With QPRC

Requirements can vary according to the property, tree, proposed work and applicable planning controls. Check current QPRC requirements before regulated tree work proceeds.

Step 3

Schedule the Work

Once the approval position is clear and any required approvals are in place, work can be scoped and scheduled with an arborist.

Important: It is important not to assume that a particular Googong tree is automatically exempt from approval or that every tree requires a permit. Requirements depend on the property, tree, proposed work and applicable QPRC planning controls. Googong also has its own development-control history and planning framework as a planned urban-development area.

QPRC had recently completed public consultation on a draft Tree Management Policy. The safest approach is to check current QPRC requirements for the actual Googong property and proposed work before regulated removal or pruning begins.
