ACT Tree Protection

ACT Tree Removal and Pruning Approvals

Do not assume that owning the property automatically means every tree can be removed. Inner North is in the Australian Capital Territory, so ACT tree-protection legislation applies. Under current ACT guidance, a private tree is protected if it meets any one of the thresholds below.

8 m
Height

Tree is 8 metres or taller

8 m
Canopy Width

Canopy is 8 metres or wider

1 m
Trunk Circumference

Trunk circumference at least 1 m measured 1.4 m above ground

A private tree is protected if it meets any relevant protected category, including being 8 metres or taller, having a canopy 8 metres or wider, or having a trunk circumference of at least 1 metre measured 1.4 metres above natural ground level. Registered trees, certain dead native trees and some replacement trees are also protected.

Approval is required for removal and certain other activities affecting protected trees. A qualified arborist can help assess the tree and proposed work, but formal approval comes from the relevant ACT Government process. Where approval may be necessary, confirm it before scheduling removal. Discuss your tree with Flora — we can help you understand what tree work appears necessary.
