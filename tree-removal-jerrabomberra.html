The correct approach depends on the species, age, condition and purpose of the work. Flora provides professional tree pruning and shaping for dead, damaged or overgrown branches, and for trees that need thoughtful management rather than removal.

NSW Tree Regulations

Jerrabomberra Tree Removal: NSW and QPRC Rules Apply

NSW rules apply here — not ACT

Jerrabomberra sits under NSW and Queanbeyan-Palerang Regional Council controls rather than ACT protected-tree legislation. QPRC's private-tree guidance expressly identifies Queanbeyan, Googong and Jerrabomberra. Do not apply ACT tree-protection thresholds to a Jerrabomberra property.

Step 1

Check Your Address

Confirm the property sits within the QPRC local government area and identify the planning controls applicable to the specific address and proposed work.

Step 2

Confirm With QPRC

Approval requirements can depend on the property, tree, proposed work and current QPRC planning controls. Property owners should confirm any required council approval before regulated work proceeds.

Step 3

Schedule the Work

Once the approval position is clear and any required approvals are in place, work can be scoped and scheduled with an arborist. Flora can assess the physical tree work.

Important: South Jerrabomberra has its own development-control context, reinforcing the need to avoid broad statements implying every property in the wider Jerrabomberra area is governed identically. QPRC's consultation on a draft Tree Management Policy closed in September 2026 — regulations and guidance should therefore be checked against the current council position rather than assumed to be unchanged.
