Know When to Act

When to Call an Emergency Arborist

Call for urgent arborist assistance when any of the following situations apply. Do not stand beneath unstable branches or attempt to cut a damaged tree yourself — storm-damaged trees can contain heavy suspended limbs that may move unexpectedly.

Tree or Large Branch Has Fallen

A private tree or large branch has come down and is on the ground, blocking access or causing damage.

Tree Threatening a Building or Vehicle

A damaged tree is leaning toward or actively threatening a building, fence or vehicle on the property.

Hanging or Partially Attached Limb

A major limb is hanging or partially attached and could fall without warning onto people or property below.

Split or Unstable Section

A split or unstable section could fail at any time, creating an immediate hazard to anyone in the area.

Tree Blocking Property Access

A fallen or damaged tree is preventing safe access to your property, driveway or a public thoroughfare.

ACT Regulations

Urgent Tree Removal and ACT Approval

An emergency does not mean tree-protection requirements should simply be ignored.

ACT Government guidance states that when a tree on private land presents a safety risk and needs urgent removal, the owner should contact Access Canberra and ask for "urgent circumstances authorisation for tree removal." Depending on the situation, approval may be provided over the phone or in person, and a photograph may be requested. Where urgent authorization is granted, a tree activity application is then required as soon as practicable afterward.

For non-urgent work affecting a protected private tree, ACT Government permission can be required before removal or pruning. Flora can help you understand what tree work appears necessary, but government approval remains a regulatory matter and should be confirmed with the relevant authority where required.

Emergency Tree Service Canberra — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about emergency tree removal, safety, ACT approval and what to do after a storm.

Do you provide emergency tree removal in Canberra?

Yes. Flora advertises urgent tree assistance and removal for dangerous, fallen and storm-damaged trees. Call first to confirm current emergency availability.

Are you available 24/7?

For urgent tree situations in Canberra — storm damage, fallen trees, or dangerous branches — call Flora Tree Services on 0480 560 030 to check current emergency availability.

What should I do if a tree is threatening someone's life?

Call 000 immediately. A commercial arborist should not be presented as a replacement for emergency services.

What if the tree has hit a powerline?

Keep away from the area. ACT Government guidance says to contact Evoenergy on 13 10 93 when a tree is touching or has brought down powerlines.

Who should I call after a storm?

For assistance making a property safe following a storm event, ACT Government guidance directs residents to the SES on 13 25 00. Flora can then assist with private-property tree work where appropriate.

Can an emergency tree be removed without normal ACT approval?

ACT Government has an urgent-circumstances process. For a private tree requiring urgent removal because of a safety risk, call Access Canberra on 13 22 81 and request urgent circumstances authorization.

Can a storm-damaged tree be saved instead of removed?

Sometimes. The appropriate solution depends on the remaining structure and damage. Pruning damaged material may be possible where full removal is not necessary.

Do you remove the stump as part of emergency removal?

Stump grinding can be arranged where required, but it should normally be treated as a separate scope item unless the quote specifically includes it. Flora already provides stump grinding as a service.
