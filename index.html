About Flora Tree Services - Canberra Arborists

Flora Tree Services is a locally owned and fully insured arborist company providing professional tree removal, tree pruning & trimming, hedge trimming, stump grinding, and garden maintenance across Canberra, Queanbeyan, and surrounding suburbs.

Our priority is professional tree work - from safe removal of large or dangerous trees to precise tree pruning and hedge trimming. We also provide complete garden maintenance services, ensuring your property always looks its best.
