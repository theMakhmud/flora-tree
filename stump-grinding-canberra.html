What We Do

Why Choose Stump Grinding?

Stump grinding uses specialist machinery to progressively reduce the visible stump and root flare into smaller material. For many residential sites, it can be considerably less disruptive to the surrounding ground than excavating an entire stump and root mass.

  • Less disruptive than full excavation
  • Removes obstacle for mowing, paving and garden redesign
  • Depth matched to your end use (lawn, landscaping, construction)
  • Suitable for residential and commercial sites
  • Can be combined with tree removal or quoted as a standalone job

Stump Grinding vs Full Stump and Root Removal: Stump grinding should not be confused with excavating every major root from the ground. Grinding primarily reduces the stump and accessible root flare, while larger roots can remain below the soil and break down over time. If you are planning a driveway, retaining wall, building footing, pool or another project where remaining roots or excavation depth matter, mention that before work begins so the required scope can be assessed properly.

Pricing

What Affects the Cost of Stump Grinding in Canberra?

Pricing should be site-specific rather than based on an arbitrary one-price-fits-all figure. We assess each job individually and provide a clear scope before work begins.

What affects the price

  • Stump diameter and height — larger stumps require more time and machine passes.
  • Species and root flare — hardwood species and wide root flares affect grinding scope.
  • Required grinding depth — deeper grinding for construction or paving adds to scope.
  • Number of stumps — multiple stumps on the same site may affect overall pricing.
  • Slope and access — steep blocks, narrow gates and hard surfaces can increase complexity.
  • Surrounding work area — nearby hard surfaces, fences and garden beds affect setup.

Typical scope guide

Every job is different. As a general guide:

Small stumps Good access, straightforward depth
Medium stumps Moderate root flare or restricted access
Large + tight-access Deep grinding, hard access, construction depth

The quickest way to get an accurate estimate is to send photos of the stump, an approximate diameter, and a photo of the narrowest access point.

FAQ

Stump Grinding Canberra — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about stump grinding, depth, access and approvals.

How much does stump grinding cost in Canberra?

There is no sensible universal price without inspecting the job. Stump diameter, height, access, required grinding depth, roots, slope and the number of stumps can all affect the scope. Send photos and measurements for an initial assessment.

Is stump grinding the same as stump removal?

Not exactly. Grinding reduces the stump and accessible root flare mechanically; complete excavation attempts to remove substantially more of the underground stump and root system.

How deep will you grind the stump?

The required depth should be agreed according to the site and what you plan to do afterwards, such as lawn repair, landscaping or another project.

Does stump grinding remove all the tree roots?

No. Major roots commonly remain underground after the stump itself is ground. Tell us about future construction or excavation plans before work is quoted.

Can you grind a stump in a backyard with narrow access?

Potentially. Access needs to be assessed first. Send a photo and measurement of the narrowest gate or passage so the job can be evaluated.

Can stump grinding be included with tree removal?

Yes. It can be scoped with the tree removal or quoted later as a separate stump job.

What happens to the stump chips?

The required finish should be agreed in the quote, including whether grindings remain on-site for appropriate reuse or are removed as part of the work.

Do I need ACT approval for stump grinding?

Not every standalone stump job requires tree-removal approval, but work affecting a protected tree or regulated protected area can be controlled under ACT legislation. Check the property and proposed work where there is any uncertainty.
