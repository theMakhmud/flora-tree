ACT Tree Protection

ACT Tree Removal Rules in Belconnen

Belconnen is in the ACT, so current ACT tree-protection legislation applies. Under current ACT guidance, a private tree is protected if it meets any one of the thresholds below.

8 m
Height

Tree is 8 metres or taller

8 m
Canopy Width

Canopy is 8 metres or wider

1 m
Trunk Circumference

Trunk circumference at least 1 m measured 1.4 m above ground

A private tree is protected if it meets any relevant protected category, including being 8 metres or taller, having a canopy 8 metres or wider, or having a trunk circumference of at least 1 metre measured 1.4 metres above natural ground level. Registered trees, certain dead native trees and some replacement trees are also protected.

Approval is required for removal and certain other activities affecting protected trees. If the tree's protection status is uncertain, check it before scheduling regulated work. Contact Flora — we can help you understand what tree work appears necessary.

Site Access and the Removal Method

Whether the tree is in an open area or surrounded by other structures will affect the removal method. The removal method depends heavily on the property — open space can make a job relatively straightforward, while established residential sites present a different set of considerations.

Open Access

Open space can make a job relatively straightforward

Where a tree stands in open ground with clear surroundings, the scope is generally easier to define and plan.

  • Clear work zone around the tree

  • Access route to bring in equipment without obstruction

VS
Restricted Access

Established residential sites can include roofs, fences, sheds, driveways and neighbouring property

Restricted access and surrounding structures require a more careful assessment of the work zone before any work begins.

  • Roofs, fences, sheds and driveways within the work zone
  • Garden structures and neighbouring property to consider
  • Restricted machinery access affects method selection
  • Where whole-tree felling is inappropriate, progressive dismantling using methods suitable for the site is required
Before work begins, the practical scope should assess the tree, surrounding work zone, access, services and space available to process branches and timber. Contact Flora to discuss the site conditions at your Belconnen property.

FAQ

Tree Removal Belconnen — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about tree removal, ACT approval, pruning and arborist services in Belconnen.

Do I need approval to remove a tree in Belconnen?

If it is a protected tree under ACT legislation, removal approval is required. Check the current tree status before regulated work begins.

How do I know whether an ACT tree is protected?

Current ACT size criteria include trees at least 8 metres tall, with canopy width of at least 8 metres, or with trunk circumference of at least 1 metre measured 1.4 metres above natural ground level. Other protected categories also exist.

Should a large tree automatically be removed?

No. Size by itself does not determine the correct treatment. An arborist should assess the tree, concern and site before deciding between removal, pruning or another option.

Do you provide tree pruning in Belconnen?

Yes. Flora provides professional tree pruning across its Canberra service area.

Can stump grinding be included in the removal quote?

Yes. Ask for it to be specified in the quotation if you want the remaining stump ground after removal.

What affects the cost of tree removal in Belconnen?

Tree size and condition, site access, nearby structures and services, the required removal method, material handling and stump work can all affect the scope.

Does Flora service the wider Belconnen district?

Flora currently lists Belconnen within its Canberra service coverage.

What should I do with an urgent dangerous tree?

For an immediate danger to life, use emergency services first. If protected-tree work is required urgently, follow the ACT Government's current authorisation process rather than assuming normal approval rules can be ignored.
